Set up jobradar

This writes config.json + profile.json on your machine. Nothing here is sent anywhere yet — it's only used later when you run a scan or ask for a tailored resume.

Who you are

Used to fill the header of any resume/cover letter you generate. Never sent to an AI backend.

Your background

This is what gets matched against job postings and what your resume is built from. Be specific — a thin profile gets thin matches.

What you're looking for

Also drives jobradar.py watch's live alerts — but only for companies jobradar can find a job board for. You'll get a chance to resolve any it can't match automatically after saving.

Get alerted on new roles optional

jobradar can watch your target companies and notify you the moment a new posting appears — even while your laptop is closed, if you schedule it. This runs outside the browser, not something a web page can do for you.

cd <this folder> && .venv/bin/python jobradar.py watch

To run it on a schedule: launchd (macOS), cron (Linux), or Task Scheduler (Windows).
